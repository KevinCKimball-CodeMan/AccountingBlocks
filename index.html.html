Welcome to Accounting Blocks

This game will help you learn to classify financial accounts

as well as learn your increases and decreases and your

debits and credits.

Press ENTER TO START the game and account blocks will begin to fall.

Use your left, right, up and down arrow keys to position the falling block

over its correct account classification and its correct debit or credit effect.

The game will last two minutes, but you can press ESC to end the game early.
